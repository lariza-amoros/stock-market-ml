Project Overview
Can machine learning predict stock market behavior? More importantly, can those predictions be used to make better investment decisions?

In this project, you will develop an end-to-end machine learning pipeline to analyze historical stock market data, predict future stock returns, estimate investment risk, and simulate investment strategies.

You will compare Random Forest and XGBoost, two widely used ensemble learning algorithms, to investigate whether historical financial patterns contain useful predictive information.

Your final goal is to determine whether your machine learning models can outperform simple investment strategies, including a market benchmark.

You are encouraged to use AI assistants (ChatGPT, Codex, GitHub Copilot, etc.) to help develop, debug, and improve your implementation. However, you are responsible for understanding your code, interpreting your results, and explaining your conclusions.

Prediction targets

Target A — Future Stock Return (Regression)

Predict the percentage return over the next five trading days:

[
R_{t,t+5}=\frac{P_{t+5}-P_t}{P_t}

Target B — Future Market Risk (Classification)

Predict whether a stock will experience high volatility during the next five trading days.

Important: All features must use information available at the time of prediction. Using future observations to construct predictive features is considered data leakage.

Part 4 — Portfolio Simulation
Suggested completion: Day 3

Imagine you are managing a hypothetical investment portfolio with an initial value of $100,000.

Use your trained machine learning models to construct investment strategies.

Strategy 1 — Equal-Weight Portfolio
Distribute capital equally across all selected stocks.

This represents a simple diversification strategy.

Strategy 2 — Machine Learning Portfolio
Use predicted five-day returns to rank stocks.

At each rebalancing period:

Predict future returns for all stocks.
Select the three stocks with the highest predicted returns.
Allocate capital equally among those stocks.
Hold the positions until the next rebalancing period.
Strategy 3 — Risk-Aware Machine Learning Portfolio
Combine predicted returns with your volatility classification model.

Select stocks based on predicted returns while avoiding or penalizing stocks classified as high risk.

You must explain your selection and allocation methodology.

Backtesting requirements
Evaluate all strategies on the same untouched test period and compare them with SPY buy-and-hold.

Calculate:

Total return
Annualized return
Annualized volatility
Sharpe ratio
Maximum drawdown
Include a realistic transaction-cost assumption and avoid look-ahead bias. Predictions must be generated using information available before the simulated trade. For simplicity, you may rebalance every five trading days and use next-session executable prices.

Required visualization: A single plot showing the growth of $100,000 under each strategy, including the SPY benchmark.
